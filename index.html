What it does

Five kinds of email, off your plate

The important-but-easy-to-forget emails that slip when you're busy looking after customers.

  • Quote follow-ups

    Up to three friendly check-ins on quotes that haven't been accepted: after 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days. They stop as soon as the customer replies.

  • Invoice reminders

    Polite reminders 3 days before the due date, on the day, and at 7 days overdue. Never threatening, never adds fees. Disputes come straight to you.

  • Review requests

    One polite Google review ask after each finished job or appointment. The same ask for everyone, with no incentives, in line with Google's policy.

  • After-hours replies

    A warm "got your message" reply to evening and weekend enquiries, asking for the details you'd need. Anything you've marked as urgent is flagged to you straight away.

  • Missed-call follow-up

    When a voicemail or missed-call email lands in your inbox, it sends the caller an email in your approved wording if it knows their address. If not, you get a call-back note.

  • Your wording, approved once

    You OK each template at setup. After that they send automatically, and anything outside them comes to you first.

FAQ

Fair questions

Is it right for my kind of business?

If you're a small business (1–5 people) that runs on Gmail and sends quotes, invoices or appointment follow-ups, it should fit. That includes trades, cleaners, salons, clinics, consultants and plenty more. The templates are written around how you work and the tone you like.

Does it send emails without me?

Only emails that use a template you've approved. At setup you read and OK the wording of each one: quote follow-ups, invoice reminders, review requests, after-hours replies and missed-call follow-ups. After that they send automatically. Anything outside a template, plus prices, dates, complaints, disputes and urgent jobs, always comes to you first. You can also start with an optional first week where emails are drafted but not sent.

Will it quote prices or book appointments for me?

No. It never quotes prices, books appointments or jobs, or changes amounts. Anything about prices or dates comes to you first.

Will it annoy my customers?

It's designed not to. Quote follow-ups are friendly and capped at three, and any reply from the customer stops them. Invoice reminders never threaten or add fees. Review requests are one polite ask per finished job or appointment. Every email has a STOP opt-out, and opt-outs are honoured straight away.

Can I pause it or see what's been sent?

Yes. You can pause it any time, and you'll get a daily or weekly digest (your choice) of what went out. Every email also sits in your own Gmail Sent folder.

Do I need Gmail?

Yes. It works through Gmail, and every email is sent from your own inbox in your name. You'll also connect Google Calendar (read-only) and your Google review link.

Does it handle phone calls or texts?

Not yet. Today it's email only. It can follow up missed calls by email when your voicemail or missed-call notices arrive in your inbox. Phone and SMS are planned as an optional add-on later.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There's no lock-in. If you're not happy, you can get your first month back within 30 days (the setup fee isn't refundable).
